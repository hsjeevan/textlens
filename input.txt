ACME CORPORATION INFORMATION SECURITY & REMOTE WORK POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document ID: SEC-POL-042

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the security standards for all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors accessing Acme Corporation networks, systems, and data. Protecting our intellectual property and client data from cybersecurity threats is a top priority for our organization.

2. Password and Authentication Guidelines
All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all corporate applications, cloud storage, and email accounts. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long, containing a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols. Reusing previous passwords is strictly prohibited, and passwords must be rotated every 90 days.

3. Remote Work and Public Wi-Fi Security
When working remotely, employees must ensure their home network is secured with WPA3 encryption. Connecting to unencrypted public Wi-Fi networks (such as those in coffee shops, airports, or hotels) without using the corporate Virtual Private Network (VPN) is a severe violation of data privacy protocols. Screen privacy filters should be used in public spaces to prevent visual data breaches.

4. Incident Reporting and Compliance
Any suspected data breach, unauthorized access, phishing attempt, or lost device must be reported immediately to the IT Security Team within two hours of discovery. Non-compliance with these cybersecurity mandates may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. Regular audits will be conducted quarterly to ensure total adherence across all departments.
